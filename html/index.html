About me

I am a master's student in bioinformatics in Prof. Jialiang Huang's lab at Xiamen University. My research sits between statistics, machine learning and biology: I build and apply computational methods for single-cell and spatial multi-omics, where the central challenge is to recover reliable biological signal from data that are large, sparse and noisy.

Rather than treating analysis pipelines as black boxes, I try to understand what each step is doing and where it can fail. I care about methods that are interpretable, reproducible and honest about uncertainty — and about keeping every analysis tied back to the biology it is meant to explain.

When I am not at the terminal I play music, read, and swim.
